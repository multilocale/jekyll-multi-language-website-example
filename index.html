--- layout: home permalink: / --- {%- comment -%} No `lang:` and no `page_id:` on purpose. This page has no per-language source file: polyglot renders this one file three times, and every word on it comes out of site.data.strings. That is the pattern you want for anything that is chrome rather than prose — landing pages, navigation, forms, error pages. Compare with about.md, which does carry `lang:` and `page_id:` because its body is prose that a dictionary cannot hold. {%- endcomment -%}
